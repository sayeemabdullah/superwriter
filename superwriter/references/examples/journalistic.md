# Journalistic — example

The city will close the Anderson Street bridge for six weeks beginning Monday, the transportation department said Thursday.

Inspectors found corrosion in two support beams during a routine review last month, the department said.

Drivers will be detoured onto Route 9, adding about ten minutes to the trip, officials said. The bridge carries roughly 4,000 vehicles a day.

**Shows:** the newest, most important fact first; every disputable point attributed; one- to two-sentence paragraphs, any of them cuttable from the bottom.
