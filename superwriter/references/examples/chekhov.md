# Chekhov — example

They agreed, over tea, that selling the orchard was the only sensible course, and that it was a pity, and that pity changed nothing. Afterward Lyuba stood at the window a long while. A workman was already measuring the far row with a length of string. She noticed that one of the teacups had a chip she had never seen before, and she turned it so the chip faced the wall.

**Shows:** people discussing everything except what matters; judgment withheld from all of them; a small gesture with an object at the end, left uninterpreted.
