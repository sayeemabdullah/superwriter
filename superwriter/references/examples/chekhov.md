# Chekhov: example

They agreed, over tea, that letting the mill go was the only sensible thing, and that it was sad, and that being sad about it changed nothing. Afterward Vera sat by the stove a long while. Somewhere past the yard a man was already winding a chain onto a drum, unhurried, as if he had done it many times before. She noticed that one of the cups had a chip she had never seen, and she turned it until the chip faced the wall.

**Shows:** people discussing everything except what matters; judgment withheld from all of them; a small gesture with an object at the end, left uninterpreted.
