# Legal: example

The Recipient shall not disclose any Confidential Information to any third party except: (a) to those of its employees and advisers who (i) have a need to know it for the Permitted Purpose and (ii) are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those set out herein; or (b) to the extent required by applicable law, provided that the Recipient gives the Discloser prompt written notice where lawfully permitted to do so.

**Shows:** defined terms ("Recipient," "Confidential Information") repeated rather than pronouns; exhaustive enumerated exceptions; "shall" for obligation.
