# Wilde — example

She had the reputation of a woman who told the truth, which in society is much the same as having no reputation at all. People forgave her everything except being right, and that only because she managed it so seldom. I liked her enormously. I have never been able to say why, and I have decided that not knowing is the last honest pleasure left to me.

**Shows:** a received wisdom swapped to sound wiser than the truth; balanced antithetical clauses; sustained wit broken by one plain sentence.
