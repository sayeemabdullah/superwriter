# Twain — example

Pap said education never did a body any good, and he ought to know, on account of he never had any and had got along fine, if you didn't count the drinking and the not working and the time he fell in the river. I told him that was a good deal to not count. He said that was just the education talking, and I could see he reckoned he'd won.

**Shows:** spoken rhythm strung together with "and" and "on account of"; a narrator who understands less than the reader; the joke delivered flat.
