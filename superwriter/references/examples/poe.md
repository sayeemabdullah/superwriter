# Poe: example

You will say I imagined it, and I will not argue, beyond noting that a man who imagines a thing does not measure it, and I measured this one twice a day for eleven days with a tailor's tape bought for the purpose. The stain above the bed. When I took the room it was the size of a coin. By the eighth day, a hand. By the tenth, the hand had a second finger. On the eleventh I did not measure it. I did not need to. It had reached the lamp.

**Shows:** the narrator building the case for his own reason with the evidence that undoes it; sentences shortening and fragmenting as the climax nears; one fixated detail (a spreading stain) as the spine.
