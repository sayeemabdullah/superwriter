# Plain English — example

To close your account, sign in and go to Settings. Select "Close account" and confirm. We delete your files within 30 days. If you paid for a year and close early, we refund the months you did not use. You do not need to call us or give a reason.

**Shows:** the reader's action first; one idea per sentence; "delete" and "refund" instead of "deletion" and "reimbursement."
