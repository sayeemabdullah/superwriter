# Dickens — example

The office had been waiting since dawn, and it went on waiting in the particular way of offices — the clock consulting itself, the ledgers holding their breath, the one lame chair by the door creaking whenever a draught remembered it. Mr. Pring arrived at nine precisely, as he always arrived, and said "Well, well, well" to nobody, and hung his hat on the third peg, which was his.

**Shows:** clock, ledgers, and chair given intention; a list run past necessity; a character fixed by a single repeated verbal habit.
