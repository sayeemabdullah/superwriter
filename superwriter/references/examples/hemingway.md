# Hemingway — example

He ordered another beer and did not look at her. The train was late. Outside a man loaded crates onto a cart and the rain fell on the crates and on the man. She said it would be better in the spring. He said the spring was a long way off. They did not talk about the letter.

**Shows:** parataxis and repeated plain words; the load-bearing omission — the letter, never explained; feeling displaced onto the rain and the man with the cart.
