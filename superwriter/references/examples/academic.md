# Academic — example

Prior work has largely attributed the decline to external shocks (Reyes, 2019; cf. Osei, 2021). The present analysis suggests that this account, while not incorrect, may be incomplete: internal coordination costs appear to explain a substantial, though not readily quantified, share of the variance. This interpretation remains provisional and is offered as a direction for further study rather than a settled finding.

**Shows:** every claim attributed or hedged; nominalized abstraction ("coordination costs," "the variance"); disagreement phrased as "incomplete," not "wrong."
