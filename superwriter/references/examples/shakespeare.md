# Shakespeare — example

Speak not of patience to me, sir; patience is a coin that beggars carry and the rich have never need of. Yet — hold — perhaps I wrong the word. Perhaps to wait is not to want, but to lay the wanting by, as a farmer rests his field that it may bear the harder after. I had not thought it so till now.

**Shows:** inverted syntax (object before verb); one metaphor pursued through commerce into husbandry; the speaker reasoning aloud and reversing himself mid-speech.
