# Woolf: example

The kettle was going to boil; and waiting for it she thought how strange this was, the waiting, the mind slipping back to a morning years off when her mother had stood just here, at just this window, and said nothing that mattered, and it had all mattered, every unsaid thing of it, the whole of that vanished house.

**Shows:** narration sliding from the present into a remembered moment with no seam; a life folded into a subordinate clause; an ordinary object made to carry it.
