# Melville: example

Consider the ledger. Plainly it is a book of columns, ruled and totalled, in which a man's year is rendered as a single defensible figure. But look longer. Is it not also a kind of scripture: each entry a small confession, the whole a judgment passed in advance of any court? I have kept such books thirty years and still cannot say whether they record a life or slowly compose one.

**Shows:** dry description tipping into sermon with no transition; direct address to the reader; an object pressed until it means too much, then the meaning doubted.
