# Kafka

**Furthest from neutral:** impossible premise reported in bureaucratic calm · subordination that enacts entrapment

- **Rhythm:** steady, unhurried, unemphatic. Long sentences qualifying themselves repeatedly without becoming ornate. No acceleration even at crisis; the flatness is the effect.
- **Syntax:** densely hypotactic. Qualifications inside qualifications, concessives, hedges, conditionals. A sentence advances a proposition, admits the objection, then admits the objection to that. The structure mimics a mind negotiating with an authority that won't answer.
- **Distance:** close third locked to one consciousness, no access to any other. Knows exactly what the protagonist knows and no more. Crucially, it does not find the situation strange.
- **Selection:** procedure, hierarchy, rooms, doors, corridors, the exact logistics of trying to accomplish something. Bodies rendered clinically. No landscape, little weather, no backstory. The world extends only as far as the immediate problem.
- **Emotion:** suppressed into the procedural. The protagonist worries about being late to work while undergoing catastrophe. The mismatch between what happens and what registers is the entire engine.
- **Dialogue:** officials explain rules that resolve nothing. Polite, elaborate, unhelpful. Characters answer questions other than those asked. Conversations end without conclusion.
- **Paragraph:** long, unbroken, no white space. Dialogue often absorbed into the paragraph rather than set apart; the visual density is part of the claustrophobia.
- **Diction:** plain, formal, administrative. Legal and clerical vocabulary. Precision without warmth.
- **Figures:** nearly absent, unusual for a writer of the fantastic. The impossible is stated literally and never compared to anything.
- **Unusual:** the absence of astonishment: a man becomes an insect and the narration attends to his difficulty getting out of bed. Logic that is internally consistent and leads nowhere.

**Writing it:** state the impossible flatly in the first line, then attend to logistics. Let sentences qualify themselves into paralysis. Give officials elaborate courtesy and no help. Never signal that anything is strange. The reader supplies the horror.
