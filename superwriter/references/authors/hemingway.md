# Hemingway

**Furthest from neutral:** omission · parataxis · refusal to state feeling

- **Rhythm** — runs of short declaratives interrupted by one long sentence built of coordinated clauses that accumulate rather than subordinate. Monotone short is the caricature; the variance is the craft.
- **Syntax** — paratactic. Clauses joined by *and*, minimal subordination, few relative clauses, main verb early. A word repeated across adjacent sentences rather than varied.
- **Distance** — close but flat. Reports what is seen and done without entering the interior. When interiority appears it is stated as plainly as physical action, which makes it land harder.
- **Selection** — the key dimension. Physical action, place, weather, food, drink, the exact sequence of doing a thing. Almost never motive, psychology, backstory. What matters most is what isn't described; the omission is structural and the reader feels its shape.
- **Emotion** — entirely subtextual. Displaced onto objects, landscape, process. Characters discuss logistics while the real subject sits underneath, unmentioned. The gap is where the story lives.
- **Dialogue** — high proportion of the text. Short, repetitive, circling exchanges. Characters talk past each other. Tags are *said* or absent. People agree while meaning opposite things.
- **Paragraph** — short, frequent breaks, dialogue often single lines.
- **Diction** — Anglo-Saxon, concrete, small vocabulary used precisely. Almost no adverbs. Adjectives limited and deliberately repeated. Definite articles do heavy work.
- **Figures** — nearly absent. Literal prose. A figure when it appears is unmissable against the plainness.
- **Unusual** — the load-bearing omission: a piece organized around something never named. Plain words repeated across sentences building incantatory rhythm from ordinary vocabulary.

**Writing it:** decide what will not be said and organize around it. Report action and place; refuse motive. Use *and* instead of subordination. Repeat rather than vary. Short sentences about drinking is the caricature and is easy to spot.
