# Woolf

**Furthest from neutral:** distance that dissolves between consciousnesses · time as depth rather than sequence

- **Rhythm:** long accumulating sentences of commas and semicolons, interrupted by short ones landing like a held breath released. The long ones don't resolve neatly; they trail, gather, turn.
- **Syntax:** loosely hypotactic. Subordination present but untidy; clauses attach associatively rather than logically. Parentheses and dashes hold thoughts interrupting other thoughts. Main verb can arrive late or be suspended entirely.
- **Distance:** the defining dimension. Narration moves between minds within a paragraph, sometimes without a break, so consciousness passes from one character to another mid-scene. Also lifts into impersonal observation and descends again.
- **Selection:** perception, not event. What a character notices and the association it triggers. Ordinary objects carry enormous weight. Plot happens offstage or in parentheses. Physical description rendered through the perceiver's state, never neutrally.
- **Emotion:** in the movement of attention. Not stated but enacted by what the mind reaches for and flinches from. An object described at length is carrying something.
- **Dialogue:** sparse against interiority. Often reported rather than quoted; what's said matters less than the thought before and the misreading after. Characters rarely say the thing.
- **Paragraph:** long, associative, unified by a movement of attention rather than a topic. Breaks fall where consciousness shifts.
- **Diction:** Latinate abstraction mixed with very concrete sensory nouns. Colour, light, sound. Present participles carrying ongoing action.
- **Figures:** frequent, extending across paragraphs and returning transformed. Waves, light, flowers, windows, the sea.
- **Unusual:** time made spatial: a moment expanded to hold a life, decades passing in a subordinate clause. The unannounced transition between minds.

**Writing it:** follow attention rather than event. Let sentences accumulate without resolving. Move between consciousnesses without signposting. Lengthening sentences and adding semicolons is not the style. The distance work is.
