# Craft Dimensions

Shared vocabulary. Every profile is written against these axes. Read before any request.

A voice is a set of positions on these dimensions. Vocabulary and punctuation habits are downstream effects, not the thing. Reproduce positions and surface follows; reproduce surface alone and you get costume.

| # | Dimension | What to attend to |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Rhythm** | Not average length — the *pattern* of variance. Whether long sentences resolve or accumulate. What a paragraph sounds like aloud. |
| 2 | **Syntactic architecture** | Paratactic (clauses side by side, joined by *and*) vs hypotactic (nested subordination). Where the main verb sits. Whether the point is front-loaded or withheld. **The most reliable marker and the most often missed.** |
| 3 | **Narrative distance** | Close third in the character's idiom; cool omniscience; limited first person; free indirect blur. Whether distance holds or moves within a scene. |
| 4 | **Selection** | What is described and what is skipped. Omission is composition. Ask: what would this writer decline to tell us and trust us to infer? |
| 5 | **Emotional placement** | Stated outright; held in subtext under dialogue about something else; displaced onto an object or landscape; ironized. |
| 6 | **Dialogue** | Proportion spoken vs narrated. Punctuation convention. Tags plain, elaborated, or absent. Whether speakers have distinct idiolects. How much is left unsaid. |
| 7 | **Paragraph shape** | Length, where breaks fall, whether a paragraph builds to its last line or leads with it. |
| 8 | **Diction register** | Latinate vs Anglo-Saxon. Abstraction level. Adjective and adverb density. **Adjust this last — adjusting it alone produces caricature.** |
| 9 | **Figurative habit** | Frequency and kind. Whether images come from one consistent domain. Brief or extended. Whether prose is largely literal. |
| 10 | **Genuinely unusual** | The one or two things this writer does that most do not. What makes an imitation recognizable rather than merely period-appropriate. |

## Using these

Name the two or three dimensions where the target sits furthest from neutral. Write to those; let the rest sit ordinary. Maximizing every dimension reads as parody — real writers are unusual in a few specific ways and unremarkable in the rest.
