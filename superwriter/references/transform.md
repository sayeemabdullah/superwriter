# Transform Mode

## Governing constraint

**Meaning is fixed. Voice is variable.**

Preserve every claim, fact, name, number, date, and the argument's order, including logical relations. If the source says X *because* Y, the output must too, even if the target voice would rather imply it.

Change rhythm, syntax, distance, diction, paragraph shape, figurative habit.

A transform that sounds right but has dropped a claim, softened a conclusion, or invented a supporting detail has failed, and won't read as a failure. Fluency conceals drift.

## Procedure

1. **Read for content first.** List what it asserts: claims, facts, sequence. Hold that list.
2. **Read the profile.** Identify the two or three dimensions furthest from the source's current voice. That's the real work.
3. **Rewrite at sentence and paragraph level**, not word level. Word substitution produces caricature. Paratactic to hypotactic means actually restructuring clauses.
4. **Verify against the content list** explicitly, not impressionistically.
5. **Return the transformed text only.**

## Common drift

| Drift | Cause |
|---|---|
| A claim disappears | Target voice prefers implication; it got implied out of existence |
| A detail appears | Rhythm wanted another clause and one got invented |
| A hedge becomes certainty | Compression dropped the qualifier |
| Argument order changes | Target front-loads conclusions; sequence got rearranged |
| Tone shifts meaning | Irony added where the source was sincere, or removed where it wasn't |

The last is subtle. A sincere complaint in Wilde's voice sounds arch. That may be wanted, but if it inverts the writer's stance, say so rather than shipping silently.

## Rules

- Bad fits: say in one line what will be lost, then proceed if asked.
- Length shifts with the target's syntactic density. Expected. Never pad to match length or cut content to fit a compressed style.
- No reproduced passages from the author. No presenting output as authentic authorship.
- If the source has something the voice can't express without inventing (a modern concept, a technical term with no period equivalent), keep it plainly and note it.
- Preserve the source's headings, lists, and breaks unless asked otherwise.
